Eight scenes, one per kind of message. Each is a few lines of HTML: the scene measures the text in the deck's own font, lays everything out, animates it when the slide is shown, and rests at its final state in print.

What each scene reads

Kept equal to the code by a test · two of the eight An attribute a scene does not read changes nothing on the slide. check reports it as SCENE_ATTRIBUTE_IGNORED, because the picture looks finished and is not what was written.

Twelve checks guard a scene

rikiki check · SCENE_* codes, errors and warnings One cell per check. Eight of them mean the slide shows something other than what was written; four are writing rules, such as one emphasis per scene. Pick the shape from what the items have in common, write the words, and let the check say what the drawing would not. The picture is not a decoration, it is the evidence for the title. When it is wrong, the check should know before the room does.
