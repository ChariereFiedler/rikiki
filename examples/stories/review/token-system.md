# Story theme revision

The shared beige/orange adapters made the three decks look related despite different titles. Old `.canvas` layouts also remained in every file. Those obsolete rules are removed. Layout CSS now lives beside the artwork; `art/story-tokens.css` owns the three palettes, display/body pairs, type scales, surface hierarchy, borders, radii and native component adapters.

- Quidditch: burgundy, ivory, brass; Cormorant and Georgia; thin rules, square editorial cells. Native table compares trial questions, without fabricated ratings.
- ACME: midnight blueprint, cream, signal yellow; Anton and Courier; hard edges and offset shadows. Native metrics define rehearsal conditions, alongside graph, flow and versus.
- Three Pigs: mineral white, petrol, terracotta; Manrope and Arial; quieter rules and rounded surfaces. Native checklist distinguishes acceptance criteria from superficial completion.

Visual review caught an ACME cell retaining the old cream fill with new cream text; the theme-scoped surface adapter fixes it. Cover counter contrast and pending timeline opacity were also adjusted. New captures and thumbnails accompany this revision. These are revised proposals, not a claim of user-approved art direction. The new checker reports visual quality as unverified until a complete screenshot review is imported.
