# Story artwork

Three Midjourney illustrations supplied by the project owner on 2026-09-23. Originals retained unchanged; slides crop them with CSS background positioning (works in every engine) rather than shipping derived files. Slide typography and layout remain editable HTML/CSS. These fictional illustrations are not product specifications or construction drawings.

- `quidditch.css`, `acme.css`, `three-pigs.css` · one complete theme per deck, loaded after `rikiki/themes/rikiki.css`: private palette, semantic `--rik-*` tokens, component `--deck-*` tokens, then the few light-DOM rules a component leaves to the author.
- `fonts/` · self-hosted latin subsets under the SIL Open Font License, each with its `*-OFL.txt`: Bodoni Moda and Jost (Quidditch), Barlow Condensed and Barlow (ACME), Fraunces and Archivo (the refuge).
